\section{Conventions}
\label{app:conventions}

We work in $d+1$ bulk dimensions with mostly-plus signature $(-,+,\dots,+)$. The Riemann tensor is
$R^a{}_{bcd} = \partial_c\Gamma^a_{db} - \partial_d\Gamma^a_{cb} + \Gamma^a_{ce}\Gamma^e_{db} - \Gamma^a_{de}\Gamma^e_{cb}$
and $R_{bd} = R^a{}_{bad}$, so that anti-de~Sitter space has negative curvature. The action is
\begin{equation}
	S_{d+1} = \frac{1}{16\pi G_{d+1}}\int\dd^{d+1}x\sqrt{-G}\,\Big[R - \frac12(\partial\phi^I)^2 - V\Big]\,,
	\qquad 2\kappa_{d+1}^2 = 16\pi G_{d+1}\,,
\end{equation}
